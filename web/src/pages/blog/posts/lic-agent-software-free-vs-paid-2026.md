## What does LIC agent software actually need to do?

Most agents use software for five jobs. Before you compare any apps, check how each one handles these.

1. **Premium quotes:** an accurate premium for any plan, age, term and sum assured, with GST and rebates shown separately.
2. **Maturity and returns illustration:** sum assured, bonus and final additional bonus projections that a client can understand.
3. **Plan comparison and presentation:** two or three plans side by side, shared as a clean PDF or image on WhatsApp.
4. **Client servicing:** premium due dates, lapsed policies, maturity reminders and birthdays.
5. **Business tracking:** commission estimates and club membership targets.

Software that does all five well is rare. Many agents use one paid app for presentations and a separate diary or Excel sheet for everything else.

## Must-have features checklist (2026)

Use this checklist when evaluating any LIC agent app, free or paid.

### 1. Accurate premium calculation with GST

The app should show the tabular premium, the sum assured rebate, the mode rebate and GST separately. Clients trust a quote more when they can see how it was built. Try the [InsureKit LIC premium calculator](/premium-calculator) and compare its output with the official Benefit Illustration for the same inputs.

### 2. Maturity and bonus projections

Clients mostly ask "kitna milega?" (how much will I get?). A good app shows the guaranteed portion separately from projected bonuses, and makes clear that bonuses are not guaranteed. The [maturity calculator](/maturity-calculator) does this with plan-wise bonus history.

### 3. Presentations clients understand

A one-page summary in plain language closes more policies than a dense table. Look for plan presentations, family-mix proposals and budget-based recommendations. See [plan presentation](/plan-presentation), [family mix](/family-mix) and [budget presentation](/budget-presentation).

### 4. Client servicing tools

Renewal commission is the long-term income of an LIC agent, so servicing existing clients matters as much as selling new policies. You need premium due reminders, a lapsed policy list and greetings. InsureKit offers [client reminders](/client-reminders), a [premium due register](/premium-due-register) and [greeting cards](/greeting-cards).

### 5. Commission and club tracking

Knowing exactly how far you are from your next club level keeps you motivated. Use the [commission calculator](/commission-calculator) and the [club qualification tracker](/club-qualification) to plan your month.

### 6. Works offline and on low-end phones

Many agents work in Tier-2 and Tier-3 towns or rural areas with patchy internet. Lightweight web tools that load once and keep working are a real advantage.

## How to choose: a 3-step decision

1. **Start free for 30 days.** Use a free toolkit for every quote and presentation for one month. Note any task it can't do.
2. **List real gaps.** If the gaps are serious (for example, you need team management for 20 sub-agents), shortlist paid apps that solve exactly that.
3. **Take a trial before you pay.** Ask for a demo or trial, test it in a real client meeting, then decide.

This avoids the common mistake of buying a costly app in your first month as an agent and abandoning it later.

## A note on compliance

Whatever software you use, remember:

- Illustrations must make clear which benefits are guaranteed and which depend on future bonuses.
- Follow LIC's and IRDAI's rules on advertisements and the use of LIC's name and logo. Check with your branch or development officer before you publish marketing material.
- Treat client data carefully. Collect only what you need and keep it secure, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act.

### Are free LIC premium calculators accurate?

A good calculator uses LIC's published tabular rates and applies rebates and GST correctly. Always cross-check a sample quote against the official Benefit Illustration for the same plan, age, term and sum assured before relying on it.

### Can I use LIC agent software without internet?

Some web tools keep working once they are loaded and save data in your browser. Native apps vary. If you often work in low-network areas, test the app in airplane mode before relying on it in a meeting.

### Is client data safe in agent apps?

It depends on where the app stores data. Browser-based tools like InsureKit keep data on your device, while many paid apps store it in the cloud. Either way, collect only the data you need and keep your device locked.
