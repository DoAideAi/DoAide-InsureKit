The job of an LIC agent in 2026 is won or lost on speed. The agent who quotes a premium in thirty seconds, shows a clean comparison on WhatsApp, and never misses a renewal date closes more business than the one flipping through rate books. This guide lists the tools that matter, what each one does, and when to reach for it — every one of them free on [InsureKit](https://insure.doaide.com).

## 1. Premium Calculator — the tool you will use every day

A client asks, "Jeevan Labh, 10 lakh, 16 saal — kitna premium?" The [premium calculator](/premium-calculator) answers with GST, sum assured rebate and mode rebate shown separately, for every major LIC plan. Nothing builds credibility faster than an exact number quoted on the spot.

## 2. Maturity Calculator — show the money

Clients do not buy premiums; they buy maturity values. The [maturity calculator](/maturity-calculator) projects sum assured plus bonus plus final additional bonus, and shows the IRR so you can answer the sharp client who asks "what return am I actually getting?"

## 3. Commission Calculator — know your own business

Before you push a plan, know what it pays you. The [commission calculator](/commission-calculator) shows first-year, renewal and total commission for any plan, term and premium — including the bonus commission slabs for Star Club, MDRT, COT and TOT.

## 4. Agent Dashboard with Commission Tracker

The [dashboard](/dashboard) pulls your tracked policies and clients into one screen: active policies, total cover, renewals due this week, birthdays coming up, and an estimate of the commission your book is earning this year with progress toward your next club target.

## 5. Plan Comparison — win the "which plan" conversation

Put two or three plans side by side with the [plan comparison tool](/plan-comparison) — premium, maturity, returns — and let the client pick. Agents who compare openly close faster because the client feels in control.

## 6. Retirement Calculator — open bigger conversations

Term and endowment conversations cap out at a few thousand rupees of premium. Retirement planning opens lakh-plus annual premium conversations. The [retirement calculator](/retirement-calculator) shows the corpus a client needs, the monthly saving to get there, and maps it to Jeevan Umang, New Jeevan Shanti and Saral Pension.
