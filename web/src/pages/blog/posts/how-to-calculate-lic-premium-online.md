Every LIC premium is built from the same five ingredients: the tabular rate for your age and term, a sum assured rebate, a mode rebate, any rider charges, and GST. Once you know how they stack, you can check any quotation yourself — or get the number in thirty seconds with a [free online premium calculator](/premium-calculator). This guide walks through both.

## What you need before you start

1. **The plan** — e.g. Jeevan Labh (736), New Endowment (714), Tech Term (854).
2. **Age nearest birthday** — LIC uses the age you are *nearest* to, not completed age. If you are 29 years and 7 months, your LIC age is 30. Check yours with the [insurance age calculator](/insurance-age-calculator).
3. **Sum assured** — the cover amount, e.g. ₹10,00,000.
4. **Policy term and premium paying term** — e.g. 25 years with 16 paying years.
5. **Payment mode** — yearly, half-yearly, quarterly or monthly.

## Step 1: Find the tabular premium

LIC publishes a tabular premium per ₹1,000 of sum assured for every age-term combination. For example, if the rate is ₹48.50 per thousand for your age and term, a ₹10 lakh policy starts at 48.50 × 1000 = **₹48,500** per year.

## Step 2: Subtract the sum assured rebate

Higher covers get a discount per ₹1,000 of sum assured. A typical endowment slab: no rebate up to ₹1.95 lakh, ₹2 per thousand from ₹2-4.95 lakh, and ₹3 per thousand from ₹5 lakh up. On a ₹10 lakh policy that's 3 × 1000 = **₹3,000 off**, bringing the base to ₹45,500.

## Step 3: Apply the mode rebate or loading

- **Yearly:** 2% rebate (recent plans; older plans gave 3%)
- **Half-yearly:** 1% rebate (older plans: 1.5%)
- **Quarterly and monthly:** no rebate; monthly non-NACH modes may carry a small loading

Yearly on ₹45,500 means 2% off: **₹44,590**.

## Step 4: Add GST — the step most people get wrong

GST on life insurance is not a flat 18%:

- **Endowment/money-back plans:** 4.5% on the first-year premium, 2.25% from year two onward
- **Term plans and riders:** 18% every year
- **Single premium annuities:** 1.8%

First-year premium: ₹44,590 × 1.045 = **₹46,597**. From the second year: ₹44,590 × 1.0225 = **₹45,593**. Notice the renewal premium is *lower* — many policyholders think it's a billing error; it's just GST dropping.

## Worked example: Tech Term (854)

Term plans differ in two ways: GST is 18% throughout, and premiums vary with smoker status and sum assured band. A 30-year-old non-smoker taking ₹1 crore for 30 years might see a tabular premium around ₹12,000; with 18% GST the payable premium is roughly **₹14,160 every year** — no first-year/renewal difference.

## Checking an agent's quotation

A correct quotation should show the tabular premium, rebates, and GST as separate lines. If you see a single rounded number, ask for the breakup — and cross-check it online in half a minute. Honest agents encourage this; it is how trust gets built.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How is LIC premium calculated?

Tabular rate for your age and term × sum assured in thousands, minus sum assured rebate, minus mode rebate, plus rider premiums, plus GST (4.5%/2.25% for endowment plans, 18% for term plans).

### Why is my first-year LIC premium higher than renewal premium?

GST on endowment plans is 4.5% in the first year and 2.25% from year two. The base premium is unchanged; only the tax drops.

### What is age nearest birthday in LIC?

LIC rounds your age to the nearest birthday. At 29 years and 7 months you are treated as 30. This can change your premium slab, so some buyers save money by purchasing before they cross the 6-month mark.

### Can I calculate LIC premium without an agent?

Yes. Free calculators like InsureKit's show the exact premium with GST and rebates for every major plan, no signup needed. Verify the final figure with LIC's official quotation before buying.

### Does paying yearly save money on LIC premium?

Yes — yearly mode gets about a 2% rebate and avoids the loading on monthly modes. Over a 25-year policy that's several months of premium saved.
